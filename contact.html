What to include in your message

For a broken link or catalog issue: tell us the item name, the page you were viewing, your device type and what happened. A screenshot can help explain the problem.

For privacy or content concerns: describe the request clearly and include the relevant URL. If you are reporting content that may infringe your rights, identify the material and explain your concern.

For offer or locker problems: include the item name and a brief description of what happened. Do not send passwords, payment card details, verification codes or other sensitive information.
